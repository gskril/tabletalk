What we’re keeping.

The Regular’s elegant serif, calm hierarchy, warm paper, and clear restaurant photography. A grown-up identity that feels comfortable on an ordinary day.

What we’re bringing forward.

Shared plates, personal notes, and recommendations with a name behind them. Less of the software-product feeling; more of the reason people love going out to eat together.

Same foundation. Three different feelings.
Iteration Personality Food and social emphasis Closest fit
A · Sunday Table Unhurried and generous Big food photography; personal recommendations Comfort and everyday browsing
B · Corner Bistro Familiar and quietly confident Restaurant character; friends’ favorite places The original Regular, refined

My recommendation.

Sunday Table is the strongest match for the comforting, food-first feeling you described. Dinner at Ours brings the most personal social warmth. I’d explore Sunday Table’s visual system with that emphasis on friends’ notes. If you prefer to stay closest to the original, Corner Bistro is the cleanest evolution.
